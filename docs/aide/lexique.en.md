# Glossary

The French word shown in the app is given in brackets.

**Road trip** (*roadtrip*)
:   Your whole journey: a name, a start date, an end date and stops.

**Stop** (*étape*)
:   A town where you stop, with an arrival date and a number of days. Stops are numbered in trip order.

**Starting point** (*point de départ*)
:   The first stop of the road trip.

**Days** (*jours*)
:   Length of a stop, counting the day you arrive. Departure = arrival + number of days − 1.

**Accommodation** (*hébergement*)
:   Where you sleep during a stop: hotel, campsite, vehicle, family or « not decided yet ».

**No accommodation** (*sans hébergement*)
:   A stop still to complete: « not decided yet », or a hotel or campsite whose details are not filled in.

**Paid / To pay** (*payé / à payer*)
:   The payment status of a hotel or campsite. « To pay » means you will pay on site.

**Dashboard** (*tableau de bord*)
:   A road trip's page, which sums it all up: key figures, map, alert and stops.

**Route** (*trajet*)
:   The map that links your stops by road.

**Position**
:   Where a town is on the map. A town picked from the suggestions has a position; a town typed by hand does not.

**Draft, Upcoming, In progress, Finished** (*Brouillon, À venir, En cours, Terminé*)
:   A road trip's status. *Draft* means it has no stop yet; the other three depend on the dates.

**Owner** (*propriétaire*)
:   The person who created the road trip. The only one who can share and delete it.

**Can edit / Can view** (*peut modifier / peut regarder*)
:   The two rights the owner can give other people. See [Who can do what](../partage/droits.md).

**Read only** (*lecture seule*)
:   Label shown on a road trip you can only view.

**Installable app (PWA)**
:   A web app you can add to your home screen without going through an app store. See [Install the app](../premiers-pas/installer.md).
