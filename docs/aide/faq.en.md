# Frequently asked questions

??? question "Is the app free?"
    Yes. You just need to [create an account](../premiers-pas/creer-un-compte.md).

??? question "Which devices can I use it on?"
    Any device with a recent browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari): computer, tablet or phone.
    You can also [install it](../premiers-pas/installer.md).

??? question "Is the app available in English?"
    No, the interface is only in French. This guide gives the English meaning of each button and message.

??? question "Can I use the app without Internet?"
    No. Your road trips are saved online. Without a connection, the app shows « Pas de connexion Internet » (*No Internet connection*).

??? question "I forgot my password, what can I do?"
    Resetting is not available yet. If you can't remember your password, you can create a new account with another email address.

??? question "Why « days » and not « nights »?"
    A stop counts the **days** spent in the town, including the day you arrive. Departure is calculated like this: arrival + number of days − 1.
    For example, arriving on the 16th and staying 3 days means leaving on the 18th.

??? question "Can I record the price of my accommodation?"
    Not for now. The app only records whether the accommodation is **paid** or **to pay on site**.

??? question "My stop does not appear on the map or in the weather."
    The town was probably typed by hand. Edit the stop and pick the town **from the list of suggestions**.
    See [See the route on the map](../roadtrip/carte.md#a-stop-is-missing).

??? question "Can two stops have the same dates?"
    No. Stops follow each other without overlapping. The departure day of one stop can, however, be the arrival day of the next.

??? question "How do several people travel on the same road trip?"
    The owner [shares the road trip](../partage/partager.md) with each person's email. Each person must have an account first.

??? question "What about the Profil, Paramètres and Aide pages?"
    They are coming in a future version. For now, they show « Bientôt disponible » (*Coming soon*).

??? question "I deleted a road trip by mistake. Can I get it back?"
    No, deleting is permanent, for you and for the people it was shared with.
