# Problems and solutions

| What you see | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **« Pas de connexion Internet »** (*No Internet connection*) | Your device is not connected. | Check Wi-Fi or mobile data, then click <span class="ui">Réessayer</span> (*Try again*). |
| « Connexion impossible : rien n'a été enregistré. » (*Cannot connect: nothing was saved.*) | The connection dropped while saving. | Check your connection and click the save button again. |
| « Votre session a expiré. Reconnectez-vous. » (*Your session has expired. Log in again.*) | You did not use the app for a long time. | Log in again: you return to the page you were on. |
| « Trop de tentatives. Réessayez dans 15 minutes. » (*Too many attempts. Try again in 15 minutes.*) | 5 wrong passwords in a short time. | Wait 15 minutes. |
| **« Roadtrip introuvable »** (*Road trip not found*) | The road trip was deleted, or your access was removed. | Click <span class="ui">Mes roadtrips</span> (*My road trips*) to go back to your list. |
| « Vous pouvez seulement consulter ce roadtrip. » (*You can only view this road trip.*) | You have the **Peut regarder** right (*Can view*). | Ask the owner for the **Peut modifier** right (*Can edit*). |
| « Seul le propriétaire peut partager ce roadtrip. » (*Only the owner can share this road trip.*) | Only the creator of the road trip can share it. | Ask them to add the person. |
| « Cette étape a été modifiée par une autre personne. » (*This stop was changed by someone else.*) | Someone changed the stop at the same time as you. | Click <span class="ui">Recharger</span> (*Reload*), then make your change again. |
| « Météo indisponible pour le moment. » (*Weather unavailable for now.*) | The weather service is not answering. | Try again later. |
| « Carte indisponible pour le moment. » (*Map unavailable for now.*) | The map background does not load. | Check your connection. The list of stops is still shown. |
| **« Page introuvable »** (*Page not found*) | The link is wrong or the page has moved. | Click <span class="ui">Mes roadtrips</span> (*My road trips*). |

!!! tip "If nothing works"
    Reload the page (**F5** on a computer, or pull the screen down on a phone). If the problem continues, log out and log in again.
