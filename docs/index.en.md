# User guide

**Roadtrip** (also called *RoadsPlanner*) helps you plan a trip with several stops: the towns where you stop, the dates, where you sleep each night and the route on a map. You can then share your road trip with the people travelling with you.

You don't need any technical knowledge to follow this guide. The first time, the easiest way is to read the pages in order.

!!! info "The app is in French"
    The RoadsPlanner interface is only available in French. In this guide, buttons and messages are written as they appear on screen, in French, followed by their English meaning, for example <span class="ui">+ Nouveau roadtrip</span> (*New road trip*).

The guide has four parts:

- [Getting started](premiers-pas/creer-un-compte.md): create an account, log in, find your way around;
- [Plan a road trip](roadtrip/creer.md): create the trip, add stops, choose where to sleep;
- [Travel together](partage/partager.md): share a road trip with your travel companions;
- [Help](aide/faq.md): frequently asked questions, common problems and glossary.

## To get started

1. [Create an account](premiers-pas/creer-un-compte.md) with your first name, your email and a password.
2. Click <span class="ui">+ Nouveau roadtrip</span> (*New road trip*) and give it a name and dates ([Create a road trip](roadtrip/creer.md)).
3. Add a first stop: a town, an arrival date and a number of days ([Add a stop](roadtrip/ajouter-une-etape.md)).
4. Say where you sleep: hotel, campsite, vehicle, family… ([Choose where to sleep](roadtrip/hebergement.md)).
5. Open the map to see your route ([See the route](roadtrip/carte.md)).

Roadtrip works in a web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari), on a computer as well as on a phone. You can also install it like an app: see [Install the app](premiers-pas/installer.md).

!!! warning "An Internet connection is required"
    Your road trips are saved online. Without Internet, the app shows « Pas de connexion Internet » (*No Internet connection*) and you can't view or save anything.
