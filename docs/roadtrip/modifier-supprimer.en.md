# Edit or delete

Everything you entered can be changed.

!!! info "Who can edit?"
    These actions are reserved for the road trip's owner and for people with the **Peut modifier** right (*Can edit*). See [Who can do what](../partage/droits.md).

## Edit a road trip

1. On the dashboard, click <span class="ui">Modifier</span> (*Edit*).
   You can also use the **Modifier** link in *Mes roadtrips* (*My road trips*).
2. Change the name, the dates or the description.
3. Click <span class="ui">Enregistrer</span> (*Save*). The message « Roadtrip modifié. » (*Road trip updated.*) confirms the change.

!!! warning "Shortening the dates"
    If a stop ends up outside the new dates, the app refuses. For example: « L'étape 3 · Chamonix (19–22 oct.) sort de ces dates. Modifiez d'abord l'étape. » (*Stop 3 · Chamonix falls outside these dates. Edit the stop first.*)
    First move or delete the stop, then change the road trip's dates.

## Edit a stop

1. Open the stop by clicking it on the dashboard.
2. Click <span class="ui">Modifier</span> (*Edit*).
3. Make your changes: town, dates, where you sleep.
4. Click <span class="ui">Enregistrer les modifications</span> (*Save changes*).

If someone else edited this stop at the same time as you, the app shows: « Cette étape a été modifiée par une autre personne. » (*This stop was changed by someone else.*) Click <span class="ui">Recharger</span> (*Reload*) to see the latest version, then make your change again.

## Edit or delete accommodation

On the stop's page:

- <span class="ui">Modifier l'hébergement</span> (*Edit accommodation*) reopens the form;
- <span class="ui">Supprimer l'hébergement</span> (*Delete accommodation*) removes the accommodation, but **the stop is kept**;
- <span class="ui">Marquer comme payé</span> (*Mark as paid*) changes the accommodation from « À payer » (*To pay*) to « Payé » (*Paid*).

## Delete a stop

1. Open the stop.
2. Click <span class="ui">Supprimer</span> (*Delete*).
3. A window asks **« Supprimer l'étape N · Town ? »** (*Delete stop N · Town?*). Its accommodation will be deleted too.
4. Confirm with <span class="ui">Supprimer l'étape</span> (*Delete the stop*).

## Delete a road trip

Only the **owner** can delete a road trip.

1. In **Mes roadtrips** (*My road trips*), click **Supprimer** (*Delete*) under the road trip.
2. The window **« Supprimer « *name* » ? »** (*Delete « name »?*) reminds you how many stops will be erased.
3. Confirm with <span class="ui">Supprimer le roadtrip</span> (*Delete the road trip*).

!!! danger "Permanent action"
    Deleting a stop or a road trip **cannot be undone**. It also applies to **everyone** the road trip is shared with.
    In every confirmation window, the <span class="ui">Annuler</span> button (*Cancel*) is selected by default: pressing Enter or Escape deletes nothing.
