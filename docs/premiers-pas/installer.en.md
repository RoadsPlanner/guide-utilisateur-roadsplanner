# Install the app

Roadtrip is an **installable web app**: you can add it to your home screen or to your computer and open it like an app, without going through the browser. Installing is optional and free, and does not go through an app store.

The installed app is called **RoadsPlanner** and opens in its own window.

=== "Computer (Chrome or Edge)"

    1. Open the app in Chrome or Edge and log in.
    2. In the address bar, click the **Install** icon (a small screen with an arrow).
    3. Confirm with **Install**.

    The app appears in the Start menu (Windows) or in Launchpad (Mac).

=== "Android (Chrome)"

    1. Open the app in Chrome.
    2. Tap the **⋮** menu at the top right.
    3. Choose **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.

=== "iPhone and iPad (Safari)"

    1. Open the app in **Safari**.
    2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
    3. Choose **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

!!! info "There is no « Install » button in the app"
    Installing is only done through your browser's option, as described above.

!!! warning "Internet is still needed"
    Even once installed, the app needs a connection. Without a network, it shows the **« Pas de connexion Internet »** screen (*No Internet connection*) with a <span class="ui">Réessayer</span> button (*Try again*). Your road trips are not available offline.
